About FisherMen JNK

FisherMen JNK is a media company focused on printing, publishing, and digital content. Our mission is to support local communities and give emerging creatives access to professional media expertise.
The "JNK" in our name comes from our founders: the “J” stands for Jafta, and the “K” stands for Katlego.
Founded in 2019 by Jafta Masemola and Katlego M. Masemola, FisherMen JNK was created to bring professional creative standards into rural areas and townships.
Outside of work, I stay sharp through competitive chess, bringing strategic thinking into everything we build.
At the end of the day, building a lasting business takes hard work and execution.

How has the organization grown since you started?

When we started, we had huge ambition but very little market experience. We jumped in too fast without understanding the industry, which led to a lot of tough lessons.
Since then, we’ve been steadily refining our approach—understanding our audience and improving how we deliver our work. Mistakes were made, but our core vision hasn't changed.
It’s been a real lesson in resilience, and we're still actively building and improving as we go.

What key experiences shaped your work ethic, and how do they guide you today?

Calculated Risk and Resilience.

Fear of failure holds too many people back. Taking hits early on taught me to work efficiently with whatever resources we have.
We turned those challenges into our strengths.
Why are we so hands-on out in public?
It's about building a real presence. We are actively growing our brand while delivering solid work.
Public spaces aren't just for hanging out—they are our workspace, and interacting directly with real people keeps our content authentic.

What role does humor play in your work and style?

Humor shows resilience, sharp thinking, and personality. Our content uses observational satire to comment on everyday life.
On camera, I bring high energy to my delivery.
While I'm always refining my style, staying genuine is non-negotiable.

When did you realize your work had an impact beyond just entertainment?

I realized it when I was out in public, expecting to blend in, and someone recognized me and called out, “Colog-ne!” It was unexpected, but it showed how far our reach actually goes.
What makes us proudest is inspiring our audience. People often ask us about our work and its impact, which reminds us of our responsibility to mentor others well.

Besides digital media and comedy, you’re also an author. What made you start publishing?

Writing is how I process ideas. With so many creative and strategic thoughts constantly running through my head, writing helps me organize them.
At my core, I consider myself a professional writer navigating modern media. I have also written books.

What core themes do you explore in your books, and why do they matter?

Each book explores different themes like redemption, hardship, self-reflection, and personal accountability.
For example, Infinite Thoughts dives into the personal growth journey—understanding that to navigate our own lives, we first need to understand human nature.
Empathy and awareness are key to bringing people together.

Katlego M. Masemola

Do you see a conflict between your comedic work and your writing?

Not at all. Writing deeply actually gives my comedy more balance. Combining lighthearted public content with serious, thoughtful writing is a deliberate mix in my work.

What keeps you motivated when growth is slow or attention is low?

Our dedicated audience. Our motto, “Your response is our influence,” drives everything we do. Regardless of numbers in the short term, people still value what we offer, and we still have big goals to achieve.

What core ethical principles guide your content and brand?

Integrity and respect. We strictly avoid exploiting or disrespecting our audience, especially when covering sensitive topics.
While media is entertainment, it affects real lives.
If upholding high standards means growing a bit slower, that’s a trade-off we’re happy to make.

How do you stay focused during periods of pressure or criticism?

By staying focused on the main goal. Criticism and external noise are just part of the process.
Playing competitive sports taught me to push through tough moments and stay disciplined.
We are still at the very beginning of our journey.

What is the plan for FisherMen JNK over the next year?

Upgrading our video and audio gear, expanding our digital marketing, and refining our publishing pipeline.
We are focused on growing from an emerging team into an industry leader.

What long-term impact do you want FisherMen JNK to have on local communities?

We want to support young people facing tough circumstances or career doubt, proving that you can overcome local limitations.
Our mission is to bring media expertise and opportunities out of big cities and directly into rural areas and townships.

Beyond entertainment, what legacy do you want to leave?

A lasting intellectual and creative impact through literature and storytelling. Projects like Ramblings on YouTube, along with books like Infinite Thoughts and Stofi: Chaos of Entropy (on Visionary Writings), show what FisherMen JNK can do.
We want to create work with real depth and substance.

What is the biggest misconception about FisherMen JNK?

People often assume we’re a fishing business! We are strictly a media and publishing enterprise offering editing, copywriting, and creative brand promotion.
We also focus heavily on giving local artists a platform to showcase their work.

What advice would you give to young creators trying to establish themselves?

Action beats talking. Doing the work is the only way to learn and build real confidence.
Take smart risks, start executing, and build.
